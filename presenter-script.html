Demo script — Connected Bill Retrieval → Priority Payments → Pay

From twelve vendor logins to one connected, monitored, paid bill

⏱ ~9 min full walkthrough 🔗 Open the live prototype
The 30-second pitch, before you click anything Today, most AP teams manually log into a dozen vendor portals every month just to find bills — and even after they find them, getting them paid on time takes another set of logins and follow-ups. What I'm about to show you is what happens when that entire chain — retrieve, automate, approve, pay — lives in one connected system, with your team's judgment built in at exactly the moments that matter, and nowhere else.
Short on time? Hit just these 8 moments

The 3-minute version

  1. Home (empty) → Connect provider → search → select → Next
  2. Pick an authentication method → Next (mention the MFA options, don't click through all of them)
  3. Finish the wizard fast (email → login → vendor → enroll → confirm) and land back on the connected Home
  4. Click the Priority Payments tab — point out every provider's status at a glance
  5. Click Set Up on Comcast, skip straight to Payment Rules — toggle Approval Workflow on
  6. Enroll → back on Home, Comcast now shows APPROVAL NEEDED — click it
  7. On the Time Sensitive tab, approve the flagged invoice — show the warning banner, then "Got it!"
  8. Land on AvidPay, click a batch row to show the proof-of-payment panel — end here

Act 1

Connect a bill provider

The pain: someone on the AP team is logging into this vendor's portal by hand every billing cycle, just to find the PDF.

  1. 1
    Start on the empty Home screen. open
    Say"This is a brand-new account — nothing's connected yet. Watch how little it takes to fix that."
  2. 2
    Click Connect provider, search by login URL, click Search, select the result, click Next.
    Say"We search the way you already think about this vendor — by the URL you log into today."
  3. 3
    On the authentication screen, pick Email, click Next.
    Say"Every vendor secures their portal a little differently — email code, authenticator app, SMS, sometimes nothing at all. We handle whichever one you're handed."

    If asked about MFA-less providers: mention that path skips straight to the login step — no need to demo it live.

  4. 4
    Pick an email from the Forward emails to dropdown, click Next.
    Say"One-time codes get forwarded straight into AvidXchange's secure inbox — nobody on your team babysits a shared mailbox waiting for a login code."
  5. 5
    Click Submit on the login form (twice — it fills demo credentials first), let it connect.
    Say"We store this credential once, encrypted. This is the last time anyone types it."
  6. 6
    Check two or three vendors on the Master vendor selection screen, click Next.
    Say"One login often covers several of your entities or accounts under this same provider — map all of them in one pass instead of repeating this setup."
  7. 7
    Click Link on a row, pick an account and billing cycle in the modal, click Link, then Enroll.
    Say"You're confirming exactly how often this bill should sync before it ever goes live — no surprises."
  8. 8
    Confirm the enrollment modal, click Enroll.
    Say"One click, and it's live."
The payoff You're back on Home — and this provider (plus a few others already set up) now shows as fully connected. Nobody will log into that portal again. Every future bill just shows up.
Act 2

Turn on automated, time-sensitive payment

The pain: retrieving the bill doesn't pay it. Somebody still has to notice it's due, decide how to pay it, and not miss the date.

  1. 1
    Click the Priority Payments tab.
    Say"Here's every connected provider's payment status at a glance — who's fully automated, and who still needs setup."
  2. 2
    Click Set Up next to a provider that isn't enrolled yet.
    Say"Turning this on takes about the same five minutes we just spent connecting the bill in the first place."
  3. 3
    Select a vendor account, click Next, then click Validate Address and wait a few seconds.
    Say"We're checking your remittance details against what the provider has on file, live — so a payment never bounces over a stale address."
  4. 4
    Click a bank card to select it, click Next.
    Say"Pick which account funds this vendor's payments. You can wire different vendors to different accounts."
  5. 5
    Toggle Approval Workflow on, pick a workflow, click Next.
    Say"This is the control finance leaders ask for first: fully automatic, or routed through your team's approval before anything moves. You decide, per vendor."
  6. 6
    On Review and Enroll, optionally show Edit Enrollment or Add Memo, then click Enroll and confirm.
    Say"Nothing here is locked in blind — review it, leave your team a note, and enroll."
The payoff Back on Home, this provider's status has changed — it now needs one thing before its first automated payment: a quick human approval. That's Act 3.
Act 3

Approve with confidence, then get paid

The moment that matters most: this is where automation meets your team's judgment, on purpose.

  1. 1
    Click the APPROVAL NEEDED pill on Home.
    Say"This isn't a separate tool you have to go find — it's one click from the same place you were just standing."
  2. 2
    Point out the Time Sensitive Payment banner and tab.
    Say"AvidXchange already knows this needs eyes on it today — nobody has to remember to check."
  3. 3
    Click View Invoice on an ordinary row.
    Say"Most invoices sail through clean — nothing flagged, nothing to review."
  4. 4
    Go back, then click Approve Invoice on a flagged row — read the warning banner out loud.
    Say"But when something looks off — a duplicate bill, a charge that's unusually high — we don't silently pay it, and we don't silently block it either. We surface it, right here, so your team makes the call in seconds instead of finding out after the money's already gone."
  5. 5
    Click Save, then Got it! on the confirmation.
    Say"Approved, logged, and moving to payment — fully auditable."
  6. 6
    Click a View Payment link, landing in AvidPay.
    Say"And once it's paid, here's everything about that payment in one place."
  7. 7
    Click any batch row to open its detail panel.
    Say"Dates, invoice breakdown, proof of payment, full history — everything you need for reconciliation or an audit, with nothing to go dig up."
The close Ask: "From an unconnected vendor to a retrieved, automated, reviewed, and paid bill — how much of that is your team doing by hand today?" Let the silence land before you move to the ask below.

Bring it home

Everything you just watched — connect, automate, approve, pay — is one continuous system, not four separate tools stitched together after the fact. Your team only shows up where judgment actually matters: choosing a workflow, and reviewing the one invoice in twenty that looks different.

"Which vendor is costing your team the most manual time today? Let's connect that one first."

If you get a tough question

Answer what the demo actually shows. For anything below, it's fine to say "let me bring in the right person for the specifics" rather than guess.

"How secure is the stored login credential?"
Speak to what you've confirmed with security/engineering, not what seems plausible — don't improvise encryption or compliance specifics live.
"What if the vendor changes their password?"
Not covered in this prototype — flag it as a question for the product team rather than inventing a re-auth flow on the spot.
"Can more than one person approve a payment?"
The demo shows a single approver's view. If multi-step approval chains come up, confirm the real workflow options with product rather than assuming.
"What does this cost / how is it priced?"
Redirect to sales/account leadership — pricing isn't part of this walkthrough.
